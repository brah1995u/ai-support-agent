# Baggage

## Included in every ticket
- 1 hand bag up to **10 kg** (fits under the seat or in the overhead rack).
- 1 suitcase up to **23 kg** in the luggage hold (bus) or luggage rack (train).

## Extra baggage
- A second suitcase costs **8 EUR** when added online, or **12 EUR** at the station.
- Maximum 2 suitcases per passenger.

## Special items
- **Bicycles**: allowed on trains in the bike area (5 EUR, must be booked). On buses only folded bicycles in a bag, counted as a suitcase.
- **Skis and surfboards**: count as a suitcase if they are in a cover.
- **Musical instruments**: small instruments as a hand bag; large instruments need an extra seat.

## Lost items
Report lost items within 30 days. The support assistant can open a lost-item ticket with the trip details and a description.

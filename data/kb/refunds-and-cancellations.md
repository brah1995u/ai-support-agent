# Refunds and cancellations

Nimbus Travel sells two fares: **Basic** and **Flex**. What you get back depends on the fare and on how early you cancel.

## Flex fare
- Cancel up to 2 hours before departure: **100% refund** to the original payment method.
- Cancel less than 2 hours before departure: **50% refund**.
- After departure (no-show): no refund.

## Basic fare
- Cancel at least 7 days before departure: **refund minus a 10 EUR fee**.
- Cancel between 7 days and 24 hours before departure: **50% refund**.
- Cancel less than 24 hours before departure or no-show: **no refund**.

## How refunds are paid
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5–10 business days**.
- We do not refund in cash and we cannot send a refund to a different card.
- If Nimbus Travel cancels the trip, every fare gets a **100% refund** automatically, no request needed.

## How to cancel
Cancel in "My bookings" on the website with the booking code and the email used for the booking, or ask the support assistant to open a cancellation ticket.

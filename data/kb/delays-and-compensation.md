# Delays and compensation

If a Nimbus trip arrives late at the final destination:

| Arrival delay | Compensation |
|---|---|
| 30–59 minutes | 25% of the ticket price |
| 60–119 minutes | 50% of the ticket price |
| 120 minutes or more | 100% of the ticket price |

- Claim within **30 days** after the trip. The support assistant can open a compensation ticket with the booking code.
- Compensation is paid to the original payment method.
- No compensation is paid when the delay is caused by extreme weather or by an official authority.
- If a delay makes you miss a connecting Nimbus trip booked in the same booking, we rebook you on the next trip for free.

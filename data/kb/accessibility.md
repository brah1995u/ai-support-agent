# Accessibility and special assistance

- Every Nimbus train has wheelchair spaces; most buses have a lift. Book the wheelchair space at least **48 hours** before departure.
- Free **assistance at the station** (boarding help, luggage help) can be requested up to 48 hours before departure.
- A companion of a passenger with a recognised disability of 65% or more travels with a **50% discount** on the same trip.
- Guide and assistance dogs travel for free.

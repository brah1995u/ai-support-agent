# Travelling with pets

- **Small pets** (cats, small dogs) up to **8 kg** including the carrier travel for **5 EUR**. The pet must stay in a closed carrier on your lap or at your feet.
- **Large dogs** are allowed on trains only, with a muzzle and a lead, for **15 EUR**. Large dogs are not allowed on buses.
- **Guide and assistance dogs** always travel for free on every bus and train.
- Maximum one pet per passenger. Add the pet when booking or later in "My bookings".

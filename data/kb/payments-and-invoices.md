# Payments and invoices

- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Bizum.
- Prices on the website are final and include VAT.
- An invoice can be requested for any booking within 12 months after the trip. The support assistant can open an invoice ticket; you need the booking code and the tax details (name or company, tax ID, address).
- If you were charged twice, do not pay again: open a payment ticket and we refund the duplicate within 5 business days.

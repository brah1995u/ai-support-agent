# Changing a booking

## Flex fare
- Change the date or time for free up to 2 hours before departure. If the new trip is more expensive, you pay the difference.
- You can change the passenger name once, for free.

## Basic fare
- Date or time changes cost **15 EUR per passenger** plus any fare difference, and are possible up to 24 hours before departure.
- Passenger name changes are not possible on Basic fares.

## Route changes
A change of origin or destination is not a change: cancel the booking under the refund rules and buy a new ticket.

## Upgrading
You can upgrade a Basic booking to Flex for the price difference until 24 hours before departure.

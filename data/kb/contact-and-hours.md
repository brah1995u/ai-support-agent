# Contact and support hours

- The AI support assistant on this website answers 24/7.
- Human agents work **every day from 08:00 to 22:00 (Spanish time, CET/CEST)**. Outside these hours the assistant opens a ticket and a human replies the next morning.
- Urgent problems on the day of travel (missed bus, accident, lost child) are always passed to a human agent.
- Nimbus Travel never asks for card numbers, passwords or one-time codes in chat.
